# July look mechanics

July is a compact, front-facing red Shiba Inu with a cream muzzle and chest, triangular ears, curled tail, black oval eyes, and a small black nose. Keep the feet/lower torso anchored at the existing idle baseline and preserve the warm orange, cream, black, and white palette.

The gaze is led by whole-face and head orientation, with both eye placement and eyelids following naturally. The ears and muzzle turn with the head; the curled tail remains behind the torso and may lag only subtly. Do not rotate or skew the whole dog, add new eye shapes, props, effects, shadows, or scenery.

Cardinal pose families (viewer/screen coordinates):

- `000 up`: muzzle and pupils rise slightly, eyelids open upward, head tips subtly upward while feet remain fixed.
- `090 right`: July's nose and pupils shift clearly toward screen-right; show slightly more of the screen-left cheek/ear and turn the muzzle right.
- `180 down`: muzzle and pupils lower, eyelids angle downward, head dips gently toward the paws.
- `270 left`: July's nose and pupils shift clearly toward screen-left; show slightly more of the screen-right cheek/ear and turn the muzzle left.

Each 22.5-degree step is an even interpolation of head turn, eyes, muzzle, and ears. Preserve size, baseline, curled-tail placement, and friendly expression across the full clockwise loop.
